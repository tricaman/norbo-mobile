Norbo is the app to organize your pets' lives in one place.

Vaccines, vet visits, parasite treatments, weight, photos and expenses: everything at hand, with reminders arriving at the right moment. No more scattered notes, missed deadlines, or lost receipts.

FOR ANY ANIMAL
Dogs, cats, rabbits, hamsters, ferrets, parrots, canaries, freshwater and saltwater fish, turtles, snakes, geckos, frogs, stick insects, horses, donkeys, goats, chickens. Norbo is built from day one for any species, not as a dog-and-cat app with the rest tacked on later.

SMART REMINDERS
Schedule the next vaccine, parasite treatment, vet appointment, aquarium water change, or terrarium cleaning. Norbo notifies you at the right time, and one tap on the push notification marks it done — no need to open the app.

COMPLETE HEALTH TIMELINE
Track vaccines, vet visits, treatments, weight, water parameters, feeding, allergies, medications. Every event has photos, notes, attachments, and cost. When someone asks "which vaccine did they get?" or "how much did they weigh six months ago?" you find it in two seconds.

PHOTO ALBUM
All your pet's photos in one place, organized chronologically.

EXPENSE TRACKING
Vet, food, accessories, grooming: discover what you're really spending, per pet and per category.

CARE GUIDES
Handcrafted care information for every animal category. Not a substitute for your vet, but a helping hand to never forget the basics.

SERIOUS PRIVACY
- No advertising
- No data sold to third parties
- Export all your data anytime
- Delete account and data with one tap

FREE
Norbo is completely free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no paywalls.

Built with care for every species.
