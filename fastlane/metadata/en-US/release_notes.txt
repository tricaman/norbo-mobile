What's new in this version:

• Reliable notifications: reminders for vaccines, parasite treatments and vet visits arrive right on time, and you can mark them done straight from the notification.
• Sign in with Apple: get in with one tap, no password to create.
• Health record: vaccines, treatments and documents always within reach.
• News section: updates and care tips, opening directly from the notification.
• Clearer expenses: see what you spend per pet and per category.
• More languages: the app follows your device language automatically.
• Stability fixes and interface improvements.
