What's new in this version:

• 11 new tools in the Services tab: birds, reptiles, amphibians, horses and farm animals now have their own tools.
• Birds: cage sizing, daily food ration and an environment guide covering household hazards to avoid.
• Reptiles: snake feeding guide, tank calculator for aquatic turtles, and an environment guide extended to tortoises, geckos, chameleons and iguanas.
• Farm & horses: chicken coop calculator, daily water needs, forage ration with a stock reminder, and a horse weight estimator.
• Amphibians & aquarium: environment guide (axolotls included) and heater sizing.
• The food & plant toxicity check now also covers birds and tortoises.
